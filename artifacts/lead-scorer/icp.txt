Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for LeadScorer
Target Company:

Industry: B2B Events, Conference Management, Association Management, Professional Services
Company size: 10–500 employees
Geography: UK, Western Europe, North America
Stage: Established organisations running 2+ events per year

Target Persona:

Title: Event Director, Head of Events, Conference Producer, Marketing Director, Partnerships Manager, Head of Sponsorship
Seniority: Manager level and above
Responsibilities: Owns event programming, speaker acquisition, sponsor relationships, or event revenue

Positive Signals (increase score):

Runs or produces B2B conferences or summits
Works at an association, media company, or professional events business
Has spoken at or organised industry events
Title references events, conferences, summits, or partnerships
Company has an events portfolio or annual conference series

Negative Signals (decrease score):

Consumer events (festivals, weddings, entertainment)
Purely technical roles with no commercial or programme overlap
Freelancer or one-off event organiser
Student, intern, or entry-level
Government or NGO unless events-focused
