Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) for LeadScorer

Target Company:
- Industry: B2B SaaS, Technology, or Professional Services
- Company size: 50–500 employees (Series A to Series C funded)
- Geography: North America or Western Europe
- Annual revenue: $5M–$100M
- Growth stage: Scaling teams, hiring actively, expanding market reach

Target Persona:
- Title: VP of Sales, Head of Growth, Director of Marketing, Chief Revenue Officer (CRO), or similar revenue/growth leadership
- Seniority: Director level and above
- Responsibilities: Owns pipeline generation, go-to-market strategy, or revenue targets
- Pain points: Struggling with lead quality, manual prospecting, or slow sales cycles

Positive Signals (increase score):
- Decision-maker or budget holder
- Works at a company with active hiring in sales/marketing roles
- LinkedIn profile with 500+ connections
- Has posted or engaged with topics around sales automation, RevOps, or lead generation
- Company recently raised funding or announced expansion

Negative Signals (decrease score):
- Student, intern, or entry-level role
- Works at a company with fewer than 10 employees or more than 10,000 employees
- Non-commercial domains (academia, government, NGO)
- No email address or LinkedIn profile available
- Title is purely technical with no commercial overlap (e.g., pure software engineer, devops)
